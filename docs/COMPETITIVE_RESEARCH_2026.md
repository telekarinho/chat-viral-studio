# Competitive research — September 2026

## Market map
### Editors / creation
CapCut, Meta Edits, Descript, Captions, VEED, InShot, Adobe Express, Canva, Submagic.

### Repurposing
OpusClip, Riverside, VEED Clips, Submagic.

### Planning / publishing / analytics
Metricool, Later, Buffer, Hootsuite.

## Important competitive change
Meta Edits is now a serious adjacent competitor: it includes in-app teleprompter, ideas/inspiration, personalized suggestions and post-performance insights. This removes “teleprompter + editor + ideas” as a defensible wedge by itself.

## White space
The strongest product opportunity is a daily operating system for an individual creator:
- understands real-world routine and availability
- converts routine into optional capture missions
- balances editorial pillar targets automatically
- has memory of concepts/metaphors/hooks already used
- asks what happened today and turns it into content
- knows what has already been captured today
- closes the loop with performance learning
- is designed around “what should I record now?”

This is materially different from starting from an editing timeline, a blank design canvas, or a social calendar.

## Product risk
Large incumbents are expanding toward end-to-end creator workflows. Meta has publicly signaled personalized Edits setup/ideas and creator assistance. Therefore we must win on creator-specific memory, daily routine orchestration, cross-platform neutrality and operational simplicity—not on generic AI editing claims.
